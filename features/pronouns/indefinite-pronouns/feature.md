# Indefinite pronouns

Pronouns that express an indefinite person, thing or quantity without an accompanying noun, as in *someone called*, *nothing changed* or *some stayed*.

## Conventions

- Compound forms in *-body*, *-one* and *-thing* count when used as pronouns, including *no one* and *no-one*.
- Independent quantifiers count: *none arrived*, *some stayed*, *both agreed*. Determiners accompanying a noun do not: *some people*, *both books*.
- Generic *one* counts: *one should be careful*. Numerals in *one book* and noun substitution in *the red one* are outside this feature's scope.
- Possessive and contracted forms count once. Mark the pronoun part: *[someone]'s bag*, *[Nobody]'s waiting*.
- Mark *no one* or *no-one* as one occurrence, including both parts.
- Locative adverbs such as *nowhere* and *somewhere* do not count.
- Noun uses and mentions of word forms do not count: *a nobody*, *nothing* named as a word.

## Notes

Feature 11 in [@biber_1988, p. 226]. The printed list is stored in `lexicons/biber_1988/indefinite-pronouns.txt`. Its entry *nowhere* is preserved there even though locative uses are outside CLEF's conventions for this feature.

The biber rule uses the listed forms without a POS test. It misses *no one* and unlisted independent quantifiers, and can count noun uses and mentions. It also misses possessives and contractions when they are kept as one token with the pronoun.
