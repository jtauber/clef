# Third-person personal pronouns

Personal-pronoun forms in the *he*, *she* and *they* paradigms, including possessive and reflexive forms.

## Conventions

- Subject and object forms count: *he*, *she*, *they*, *him*, *her* and *them*.
- Dependent and independent possessives count: *her book*, *their books*, *the book is hers*, *the books are theirs*. Both uses of *his* count.
- Reflexive and emphatic uses count: *she hurt herself*, *they themselves checked it*.
- Singular *they* and its associated forms count, including reflexive *themself*.
- Uses referring to animals and things count, as in *the ship lost her mast*.
- *It*, *its* and *itself* are outside this feature's scope.
- Contracted forms count once. Mark the pronoun part: *[She]'s*, *[They]'re*.
- Mentions of word forms do not count: *he* named as a word.

## Notes

Feature 8 in [@biber_1988, p. 225]. The printed inventory omits *hers*, *theirs* and *themself*, which count under CLEF's conventions. Biber places the separate *it* feature in C2.

The biber rule uses word forms without a POS test. It recognises the pronoun as a separate token or fused with common contracted auxiliaries. It cannot distinguish a use of a pronoun from a mention of its word form.
