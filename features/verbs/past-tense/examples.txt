# Cases where the tools' rules differ.
I [did] see her at the station.
    Emphatic *did* is a finite past form. MAT retags DO before a verb as an emphatic, so its rule misses it.
You{'d} better hurry.
    HAD BETTER is treated as a modal (see Conventions). MFTE turns *'d* before *better* into HAD, so its rule counts it.
The parcels [were] {delivered} this morning.
    A passive participle. If a tagger tags it VBD, as happens with -ed forms, MFTE's rule leaves it out while the xpos, MAT and pybiber rules count it.
They [were] {selected} and {extracted}.
    Coordinated passive participles. Even if both are tagged VBD, MFTE's rule leaves both out, so only *were* counts.
They [were] {selected} or {extracted}.
    MFTE applies the same passive correction after *or*.
They [were] {selected} and {extracted} and {transported}.
    MFTE carries the passive correction through the chain; only *were* counts.
They [selected] and [extracted] the samples.
    Two finite past-tense verbs. Without the passive context, both count.
They [had] {finished} by noon.
    A perfect participle. If it is tagged VBD, only MFTE's rule leaves it out.
He [got] {arrested} at the border.
    A GET-passive participle. If it is tagged VBD, only MFTE's rule leaves it out.
Everyone who [was] there [signed] the card.
    MFTE's rule takes an -ed form after BE and an adverb (*was there*) to be a passive participle, so it misses *signed*.
Anyone who [had] questions [asked] them at the end.
    MFTE's rule takes an -ed form after HAVE and a noun to be a perfect participle, so it misses *asked*.
What I [got] [was] a refund.
    MFTE's rule takes any past form right after GET to be a GET-passive, so it misses *was*.
She [had] to leave.
    Finite past HAVE. MFTE replaces it with its necessity-modal tag, so its rule misses it.
She [needed] to leave.
    Finite past NEED. MFTE applies the same necessity-modal correction.
She has not really {got} {arrested}.
    Both are participles. If *arrested* is tagged VBD, MFTE leaves it as past tense: *got* has already been tagged as HAVE got and cannot trigger its GET-passive correction.
