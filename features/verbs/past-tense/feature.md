# Past tense

Finite verbs in past tense form.

## Conventions

- Irrealis *were* counts: *if I were rich*.
- Contracted *'d* counts when it is HAD (*she'd left*), not when it is WOULD (*she'd leave*).
- Modal auxiliaries (*would*, *could*, *might*, *should*) do not count, though they are historically past forms.
- *had* in *had better* is treated as a modal auxiliary and does not count.
- Past time reference is neither necessary nor sufficient.

## Notes

[@biber_1988, p. 223] defines past tense through his tagger (described on pp. 214–215): any past-tense form in its dictionary, plus any word it could not otherwise identify that is longer than six letters and ends in *-ed*. He then checked the past forms by hand, separating those that function as past participles from those that function as past tense. MFTE's rule approximates that check with context patterns; the other rules rely on the tagger.
