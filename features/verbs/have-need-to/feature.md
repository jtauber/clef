# HAVE/NEED TO

Necessity expressed by HAVE or NEED with a TO-infinitival complement, as in *she has to leave* and *she needs to leave*.

## Conventions

- Include inflected and non-finite HAVE and NEED, including *had to*, *needed to* and *has had to*.
- Include negative and interrogative uses; a denied or questioned necessity still instantiates the feature.
- Count each HAVE or NEED predicate once. A shared predicate with coordinated infinitives counts once.
- Exclude possession with HAVE, NEED with a noun phrase, auxiliary HAVE in an ordinary perfect, and modal NEED without TO.
- Exclude HAVE GOT TO; it belongs to the separate HAVE GOT feature.
- Mark HAVE or NEED alone. TO and its infinitive supply context but are outside the marked piece.

## Notes

The contextual rule draws on MFTE's necessity-modal category [@mfte]. It requires TO immediately after HAVE or NEED, so intervening material can cause misses. Reusing these anchors as past-tense exclusions preserves MFTE's category choice even though finite *had* and *needed* count under CLEF's past-tense definition.
