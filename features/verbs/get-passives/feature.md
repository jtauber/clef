# GET passives

Passive predications formed with GET and a past participle, where the subject corresponds to an object of the corresponding active verb, as in *he got arrested*.

## Conventions

- Include finite and non-finite GET, including *got* and *gotten* within perfect constructions.
- Count each coordinated passive predicate separately when GET is shared.
- Exclude causative GET with an object before the participle, such as *she got the car repaired*. The car, not the subject, is what is repaired.
- Exclude GET meaning obtain, ordinary change-of-state expressions with adjectives, and BE passives.
- Mark GET and the passive participle, excluding earlier perfect HAVE. Leave intervening context outside the marked pieces.
- With a shared GET, mark it with the first participle and mark later coordinated passive participles as separate occurrences.

## Notes

The contextual rule draws on MFTE's GET-passive correction [@mfte]. It anchors on the participle and accepts supplied VBD as well as VBN. Its object gap also admits causatives, which are false positives for this feature. A finite verb after GET can also be mistaken for a participle, as in *what I got was a refund*.
