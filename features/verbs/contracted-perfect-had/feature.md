# Contracted perfect HAD

Contracted *'d* or *’d* functioning as the auxiliary HAD in a past perfect construction.

## Conventions

- Count each contracted HAD once, including perfect progressives and perfect passives.
- Include inverted, negative and interrupted constructions when the contraction is HAD.
- Exclude WOULD, including *she'd leave*, *she'd have left* and *she'd be arrested*.
- Exclude HAD BETTER, which is treated as a modal construction, and uncontracted *had*.
- Exclude possessive and obligation HAVE GOT, following the perfect-aspect feature's conventions.
- Mark only the contraction, leaving its subject and the rest of the verb sequence outside the brackets. Mark unresolved HAD/WOULD readings as unresolved.

## Notes

The contextual rule draws on MFTE's recovery of contracted HAD before a participle [@mfte]. Its two-adverb window and reliance on supplied tags are approximations. It does not exclude possessive HAVE GOT, so *he'd got a car* can overcount. These limitations belong to the rule, not the definition. The past-tense rule reuses its contraction anchors.
